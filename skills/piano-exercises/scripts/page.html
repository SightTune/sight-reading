Before you play each one

  1. Take 30 seconds to look: the key signature, the time signature, where each hand starts, and any rhythm you need to count.
  2. Count one bar out loud (or press Count in) at a speed you can keep all the way through.
  3. Keep going. Don't stop to fix a wrong note; the next beat matters more.
  4. Then press Listen to hear it, and play it once more.
